Summary

I am a fourth-year BS-MS student at the Indian Institute of Science Education and Research (IISER) Kolkata whose research lies at the intersection of chemistry, statistical mechanics, information theory, and applied mathematics. My work focuses on developing theoretical frameworks for understanding molecular organization, self-assembly, complexity, and the emergence of living matter using chemically realizable self-assembling systems as experimentally accessible model platforms. My long-term objective is to uncover universal principles governing organization across chemistry, physics, and biology.
